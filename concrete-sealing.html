Why Seal

Sun, Salt & Moisture Take a Toll

Unsealed concrete on the Sunshine Coast doesn't age gracefully. UV breaks down the surface over time, coastal salt air accelerates deterioration, and humidity gets absorbed into porous concrete — leading to staining, mould, and eventually cracking or spalling.

A quality sealer creates a protective barrier that keeps moisture, salt, oil, and UV from getting into the concrete in the first place.

Sealed concrete is also easier to keep clean — stains sit on top of the sealer instead of soaking into the surface, so day-to-day marks wipe off instead of setting in.

We match the sealer and application to your surface, its sun exposure, and how it's used, rather than applying the same product to every job.

Sealer Types

Matched to the Job

Clear Sealing

Protects without changing the look of your concrete. The most common choice for driveways and paths where you want protection, not a different finish.

Colour Enhancement

Deepens and enriches the existing colour and tone — popular on exposed aggregate and coloured concrete where you want the surface to look richer, not just protected.

Anti-Slip Coatings

An additive mixed into the sealer for pool surrounds, steps, and other areas where grip matters more than gloss.

The Process

Clean. Prep. Seal. Cure.

Questions

Concrete Sealing FAQs

—
How long does concrete sealer last in Queensland conditions?

Most sealers hold up for 2–5 years on the Sunshine Coast depending on the product, sun exposure, and foot or vehicle traffic. Driveways and pool surrounds in full sun wear faster than shaded paths.

—
What's the difference between clear and colour-enhanced sealer?

Clear sealer protects the concrete without changing its look. Colour-enhancing sealer deepens and enriches the existing colour and tone, which is popular on exposed aggregate and coloured concrete driveways.

—
Can you seal over old or previously sealed concrete?

In most cases, yes. We clean and assess the existing surface first — old sealer that's failing or flaking needs to be addressed before a fresh coat goes on, which we cover at quote stage.

—
How soon can I walk or drive on freshly sealed concrete?

Light foot traffic is usually fine after 24 hours, with full cure for vehicles typically taking several days depending on the sealer and weather. We'll give you exact timing for your job before we finish up.

—
Does sealing make concrete slippery?

A gloss sealer can add some slip risk when wet, which is why we offer anti-slip additives for pool surrounds, steps, and other areas where grip matters.
