The Problem

Why Paving Goes Green & Slippery

Paths, pavers and pool surrounds stay damp, and damp is what algae and mould need. In shaded spots and beside garden beds, retaining walls and stairs it builds up fast, starting in the joints and low points and spreading across the surface.

It isn't only cosmetic. Algae on paving is slippery when wet, which matters most around a pool, on entry paths and on stairs.

Pavers, in particular, show every bit of build-up. Dark patches, green film and stained joints can make a path look years older than it is.

A proper rotary clean lifts most of it in a single visit. As with driveways, some old stains lighten rather than disappear completely, and we'll tell you what to expect when we quote.

Then Protect It

Cleaning, Sealing & Grip

Cleaning gets the surface back. Sealing helps it stay that way, because a sealed surface absorbs less water. For pool surrounds, steps and shaded paths, we can add an anti-slip additive so grip is part of the finish.

Questions

Pool Surround & Path Cleaning FAQs

—
Is it safe to clean around a pool?

Yes. Pool surrounds are a regular part of the work, and we go through the area with you when we quote. We don't need the pool drained.

—
Will pressure washing damage my pavers?

Not when the pressure is matched to the surface. Paver joints and bedding sand can be disturbed by aggressive pressure, so we use a rotary cleaner that keeps pressure even and we check the condition of the joints before we start.

—
Can you make my pool surround less slippery?

Cleaning removes the algae and mould that make paving slippery when it's wet, and that fixes most of the problem. Where grip is a concern, an anti-slip additive can be mixed through a sealer. See our concrete sealing page.

—
How often should paths and pool surrounds be cleaned?

Most Sunshine Coast paths and paving need a clean every 12–18 months. Shaded or damp areas can grow algae and moss faster and may need it sooner.

—
Do you clean pavers, concrete and stamped concrete?

Yes. Pavers, plain and exposed aggregate concrete, and stamped or stencilled concrete are all cleaned with pressure set to the surface. If a stamped surface has a sealer that's failing, we'll point it out before we start.

—
How much does it cost to clean a pool surround or path?

It depends on the area, the surface and how much has built up. We quote every job individually and for free, usually within 48 hours. Send a photo and your suburb and we can often give you a good idea before we visit.
