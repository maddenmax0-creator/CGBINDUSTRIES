Local Knowledge

Same Estates, Same Driveways, Same Age

Forest Glen went up in a much shorter window than the older parts of the Coast, and that shows in the concrete. A lot of the housing here was poured within a few years of itself, which means whole streets of exposed aggregate driveways are effectively the same age and reaching the same point in their life at the same time.

That point is easy enough to recognise. The original sealer — where there was one — has given up: the stones read flat and grey instead of warm, the surface feels rougher underfoot, and loose aggregate starts showing up around the edges. It's the stage where sealing stops being cosmetic and becomes the cheaper option, because once the matrix between the stones lets go you're into repair rather than protection.

The other thing that sets Forest Glen apart from the higher, shadier suburbs is exposure. It's open here, and most driveways take close to a full day of sun. Moss and algae are far less of an issue than they are under the Buderim canopy — the problems that turn up instead are UV fade and plain surface wear.

That looks like colour drained out of coloured concrete, exposed aggregate going dull and chalky, and oil marks that soak in and bake rather than sitting on top. It's also why sealing matters more here than a clean on its own: cleaning fixes the surface, but on a sun-exposed driveway the sealer is the only thing slowing the fade.

Forest Glen FAQs

When is a newer Forest Glen driveway ready to be sealed?

Fresh concrete has to cure before anything goes over it — as a rule at least 28 days, and longer if the weather has been wet. Most driveways around Forest Glen are well past that point. The more common situation here is a driveway that was never sealed when the house was built and has been weathering ever since, which is a clean-and-seal rather than a wait.

—
How long does sealing last on a driveway in full sun?

Sealers generally run two to five years on the Sunshine Coast, and an open Forest Glen driveway with very little shade sits at the shorter end of that range. UV is what breaks a sealer down, so the same open aspect that keeps the moss away is what shortens the sealer's life.

—
Is a double estate driveway a full-day job?

A standard double driveway is usually a comfortable half-day to clean, and sealing is a separate visit once the surface is properly dry. If the driveway runs back to a shed, or the job takes in the path and pool surround as well, it stretches out — we scope that at quote stage rather than guessing on the day.
