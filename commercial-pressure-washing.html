How It Works

Working on a Commercial Site

Scoping first. We look at the site, or review photos, and agree the surfaces, access, water supply and any no-go areas before anything is booked. The quote is in writing and free.

Timing to suit. Tell us when the site is quietest. Where we can, we work in sections so bays, entries and paths stay usable.

Even results. Rotary surface cleaners clean large areas evenly, without the stripes a hand-held wand can leave across a big surface.

One point of contact. The owner quotes the job and does the work. There are no subcontractors, and the person you speak to is the person on site.

The Process

How a Commercial Clean Goes

01
Scope
Site visit or photos, surfaces, access, water supply, drainage and timing agreed in a written quote.
02
Prep
Vehicles and obstacles are cleared from the work area and heavy stains are pre-treated.
03
Clean
Surfaces are cleaned with rotary surface cleaners, in sections where the site needs to stay in use.
04
Inspect
Edges are finished by hand, surfaces are post-treated where needed, and we go over the result with you or your site contact before we leave.

Questions

Commercial Pressure Washing FAQs

—
Do you quote for body corporates and strata?

Yes. Common-area driveways, paths, stairs and entries are cleaned the same way as any other surface, and we scope them the same way: from a site visit or photos, with a free written quote. Tell us who to send it to when you enquire.

—
Can you work around our trading hours?

Timing is one of the first things we sort out at quote stage. Tell us when the site is quietest and any access limits when you enquire, and we'll plan the job around them where we can.

—
Can you clean oil-stained carparks?

We can clean carparks and loading areas, and fresh or moderate oil staining responds well. Old, set-in oil can lighten rather than disappear completely, and we'll say so when we scope it.

—
Do you do one-off cleans or regular visits?

Most surfaces need a clean every 12–18 months, and many commercial sites do fine with a one-off. If you'd like repeat visits, mention it when you enquire and we'll discuss it.

—
How long does a commercial job take?

It depends on the area, the surface and access. We scope and time every job at quote stage rather than guessing, and we'll tell you if it needs to be done in stages.
