Local Knowledge

Why Maroochydore Concrete Needs a Different Plan

Maroochydore is the commercial centre of the Sunshine Coast, and that changes the mix of work. Alongside streets of family homes there are unit complexes, shopfronts and commercial premises, so one suburb can mean a lot of different surfaces and finishes.

Sun and humidity do most of the work on concrete here. UV fades the surface and breaks down old sealer, and humid summers and shade feed mould, algae and moss. It is slow rather than dramatic, but it builds into a grey-green film along paths and the edges of driveways that a quick rinse does not shift. On the beachfront strip, salt air adds to the wear on unsealed concrete.

Commercial and strata work is a bigger part of the picture here than in the hill suburbs. Entrances, walkways, carparks and common areas are walked and driven on every day, so the buildup is a mix of tyre marks, oil and organic growth, and the job has to be planned around when the space can actually be closed off.

For those sites we quote per site once we have seen the area, or photos of it, and agree access, timing and water supply up front. A home clean is the same rotary surface cleaning we use everywhere else: an even pass across the slab rather than stripes from a hand-held wand.
