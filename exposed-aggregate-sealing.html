Why Seal

Why Exposed Aggregate Needs Sealing

Exposed aggregate is concrete with the top layer of cement removed so the stones show through. It looks great, but the cement between the stones is porous, and the surface has a lot of texture for dirt to settle into.

Left unsealed, it absorbs moisture, oil and tyre marks, and moss and algae take hold in the gaps. On the Sunshine Coast, humidity, strong UV and salt air add to the wear.

A sealer fills the pores and forms a protective layer. Water and stains sit on top of the surface rather than soaking in, so day-to-day marks wash off and the next clean is easier.

We apply Dulux Avista sealer. The right option for your surface depends on its colour, sun exposure and how it's used, and we talk that through when we quote.

Your Options

Clear or Colour-Enhancing

Clear sealing protects the surface without changing how it looks. It's the usual choice when you like your aggregate as it is and want it protected.

Colour-enhancing sealing deepens and enriches the colour of the stone, so the surface looks richer, not only protected.

Anti-slip additive can be mixed through the sealer for sloping driveways, entries and paths near pools, where grip matters more than finish. A gloss sealer can add slip risk when wet, so it's worth asking about.

Questions

Exposed Aggregate Sealing FAQs

—
How long does sealer last on exposed aggregate?

Most sealers hold up for 2–5 years on the Sunshine Coast, depending on the product, sun exposure and traffic. A driveway in full sun and daily use wears faster than a shaded path.

—
Do I have to clean the aggregate before sealing?

Yes. Sealer applied over dirt, algae or moisture won't bond properly. Every sealing job starts with a full clean and a proper dry, which is why we do both in the one booking.

—
Will sealing change how my driveway looks?

A clear sealer protects without changing the look much. A colour-enhancing sealer deepens and enriches the colour of the stone, so the surface looks richer. Because it does change the look, we'll talk it through before we start.

—
Can you seal exposed aggregate that has been sealed before?

In most cases, yes. We assess what's already there first. Old sealer that's failing or flaking needs to be dealt with before a new coat goes on, and we cover that at quote stage.

—
How soon can I walk or drive on it?

Light foot traffic is usually fine after 24 hours, and vehicles typically need several days depending on the sealer and the weather. We'll give you exact timing for your job before we leave.

—
Does sealing stop moss and algae coming back?

It slows them down rather than stopping them. Sealed aggregate sheds water instead of holding it, so moss and algae have a harder time taking hold, and what does grow is easier to clean off.
